Each day

Scripture

A verse chosen for the day, from the prophets, the Gospels and Mary’s own words.

Two or three sentences to carry with you. Nothing long, nothing preachy.

One practice

Pray a decade, sit in silence, prepare for Confession. Anchor opens each one for you.

Get Anchor

Put it on your home screen

Anchor is a web app, so there’s no app store and nothing to sign up for. It takes about ten seconds.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open faithanchor.net in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (on newer iOS, tap ••• first).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open faithanchor.net in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when Anchor offers it, or ⋮ → Install app.

Opened this from Instagram, Facebook or TikTok? Tap ••• or ⋮ and choose Open in browser first; their built-in browsers can’t install apps.
